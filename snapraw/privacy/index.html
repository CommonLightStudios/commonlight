SNAP RAW — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

SNAP RAW is a camera app that captures RAW photos. It does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. This policy explains what the app does with the access you give it.

What we collect

Nothing. SNAP RAW has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no servers of its own. The app does not send your photos, settings or any other data to the developer or to anyone else.

What the app uses, and why

  • Camera. Used only to take the photos you capture. The camera feed is shown on your screen and the pictures you take are processed on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Photos (add-only). If you choose to save to Photos, the app asks for permission to add photos. It cannot see, read or change the other photos in your library.
  • Files. If you choose to save to Files, captures are written to the folder you pick (or to the app’s own folder on your iPhone). The app only accesses the folder you select.
  • Settings on your device. Your choices (output format, resolution, save location) are kept in the app’s storage on your iPhone, and are removed when you delete the app.

SNAP RAW does not use your location and does not record it in your photos.

Optional tips

SNAP RAW has optional tips that support development. Payments are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment details or Apple ID; Apple provides the developer with aggregated sales reports only. Tips do not unlock any features. Apple’s own privacy policy covers purchases: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Sharing and third parties

SNAP RAW includes no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software, and shares no data with third parties.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete. Photos you save remain yours, in your Photos library or Files folder, and you control them.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect, and the effective date above will change.
